About Me

Innovative full-stack and ML engineer with 5+ years of experience across design, development, testing, and deployment. I build production software with strong emphasis on clear algorithms, reliable backends, and deep learning where it fits—from CNNs and LSTMs to transformers and sequence models—using Python, JavaScript, C++, C#, and related ecosystems.
